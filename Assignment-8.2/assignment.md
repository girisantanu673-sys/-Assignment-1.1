Collect at least 8 images[cite: 37].
Place each image inside a gallery item[cite: 37].
Use grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(...)) on the grid[cite: 37].
Apply width: 100% and suitable object-fit for the images[cite: 37].
Keep consistent spacing using gap[cite: 37].
Verify that columns adjust automatically by changing the browser width[cite: 37]. Submission: Responsive photo gallery and desktop/mobile screenshots[cite: 37].
