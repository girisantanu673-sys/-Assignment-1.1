                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              1. **3 Cards (Basic, Standard, Premium):** Built 3 distinct pricing cards structured in a flexbox container.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              2. **Title, Description, and Button:** Every card includes an `<h2>` title, `<p>` description, and action `<button>`.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              3. **Backgrounds & Borders:**
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * **Basic:** White background with a subtle gray border (`#cbd5e1`).
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * **Standard:** Light blue background (`#eff6ff`) with a prominent blue border (`#2563eb`).
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * **Premium:** Dark slate background (`#0f172a`) with a vibrant cyan border (`#38bdf8`).


                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              4. **Rounded Corners (`border-radius`):** Cards use `border-radius: 12px`, `16px`, and `20px`.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              5. **Display Property Practical Demonstration:**
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * **`display: block`:** Used on `.badge-block` ("MOST POPULAR" banner) to take up full available line width.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * **`display: inline`:** Used on `.tag-inline` to highlight key text phrases inside normal paragraph flow without causing line breaks.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * **`display: inline-block`:** Used on `.btn-inline-block` buttons to apply explicit padding, heights, and widths while retaining block formatting capabilities within the card layout.


                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              6. **Dimensions (`width`, `height`, `max-width`):** Cards utilize `width: 320px`, `max-width: 100%`, and `min-height: 480px`.
