**Assignment 7.3 — Responsive Product Listing**.

To fulfill all requirements, the project is structured into two clean files:

1. **`style.css`** (External stylesheet implementing `display: flex`, `flex-wrap: wrap`, `gap`, and the `flex-grow`, `flex-shrink`, `flex-basis` properties for responsiveness)
2. **`index.html`** (Sample webpage featuring 6 distinct product cards with images, names, prices, and buttons)
